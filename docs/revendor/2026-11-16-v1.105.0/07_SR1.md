## What the re-copy moved here

One case re-pinned, for SR-L1: `tests/test_draghandle.lua`, *a strip whose geometry cannot be read
falls back to the widget's attached tooltip, never the cursor*. The widget's attached fallback is
now centered **above** the strip (`BOTTOM` on the strip's `TOP`, 0, +2), where it was below (`TOP`
on `BOTTOM`, 0, -2). The case still checks the one owner (UIParent, `ANCHOR_NONE`) and the one point;
only the side moved. The comments in `modules/Bar.lua` and the same sentence in
`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `docs/module-map.md` now say above (with the two line counts the edit
moved).

The strip's own placement is unchanged: `tooltipPlace = NS.Util.PlaceTooltipBeside` still opens the
strip's, the `?`'s and the X's tooltips beside the strip (TP-AT-02, the owner's rule for every Ka0s
strip); the library's attached rule only applies when that hook cannot read the geometry.

## The title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4)

Absorb Tracker draws **no title-bar controls of its own**, so this step is skipped. Its only frames
are the three HUD bars (plain frames with no title bar) and their drag strips, which are
LibKa0s-Widgets-1.0's `DragHandle`: the strip, its `?` and its X are drawn, tinted and given
tooltips by the library (SR-L2, SR-L3), and the addon supplies only the words (`tooltip`,
`helpTooltip`, `closeTooltip`, all already non-empty) and the placement hook. The debug console, the
perf panel, the sim panel and the copy window are the library's windows. `core/SkinSetup.lua`
overrides no `icon.tint`, `close.rest`, `close.hot` or `text.link`, so the library's colors reach
the strip unaltered. No `W.HeaderButtons` adoption.

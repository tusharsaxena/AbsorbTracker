# 02 — Deviations

**Audited against:** Ka0s WoW Addon Standard **v2.38.0 (2026-09-02)**. Every section file linked from
the index was fetched and read; none is unassessed. Evidence and every command's real output are in
`03_EVIDENCE.md`.

**Verdict: minor deviations.** Nothing a player, their SavedVariables or their session can hit today.
Both gate commands are green (`luacheck .` 0/0 over 27 files; `lua tests/run.lua` 547/547, 0 skipped),
`lizard` warns on nothing, both vendored-payload diffs against LibKa0s `v1.25.0` are empty, the
options panel meets all nine `options-ui` content checks, and the `.gitattributes` body is the
canonical client-bound file. What remains is entirely **records, packaging metadata and one redundant
hook**.

**Counts.** Headline tally (roots only): **10**. Total including `derived from` dependents: **11**.
By impact — High **0** · Medium **0** · Low **10** · Info **1**. MUST failures, same basis: **6**
roots (`AT-51`, `AT-52`, `AT-53`, `AT-55`, `AT-57`, `AT-59`) and **1** dependent (`AT-61`, derived
from `AT-53`). Every MUST failure here is doc-, record- or config-only, which is why every one is
graded Low: the grade is impact, the MUST is still named in the row.

**IDs.** Prefix `AT-`, continuing from `docs/audits/2026-08-05/` (highest prior `AT-50`). No prior
deviation recurs — the 2026-08-05 set was closed on `feat/settings-revamp-v2`. New this run:
`AT-51` … `AT-61`.
